How to use this book

This is a hands-on course book. Read Chapters 1–5 first, then follow the setup chapters in order. After setup, study the chapter for each workspace your business enables. A teacher can use the practice exercises at the end of a chapter as a lab; learners should work in a training business or clearly identified practice records.

Button names below follow the source-reviewed interface. Stockroom is updated over time, and owners can customise catalogue wording. A control may be hidden by role, device, workspace, subscription, or configuration. On phones, open the navigation menu before choosing a destination. In focused settings screens, use the task search/picker to jump to a task; selecting a task does not itself save a setting.

Chapter 1: What Stockroom is for

Stockroom Business App helps a business record sales and payments, manage stocked items, and coordinate selected operational work. Depending on enabled workspaces, it can support product checkout, measured oil sales, manual payment receipts, food orders and preparation, table bills, and hotel or nightlife operations. It also includes management tools such as customers, staff access, purchasing and receiving, cash register, reports, and device configuration.

The app is useful when a business needs a repeatable record of what was sold or paid, by whom, at which branch, for what amount, and—where stock is involved—how the transaction changes inventory. It can reduce reliance on memory and loose paper by giving staff one guided transaction flow and owners a searchable record.

Stockroom is designed for offline-first day-to-day use in supported workflows. Initial registration, connecting devices, subscription checks, remote ordering, and some online services need connectivity. Businesses should synchronize when they need changes shared across devices and review the sync status before depending on cross-device updates.

Use Stockroom when its actual workflow matches the business. Do not infer specialist support from a business label. For example, a pharmacy business type can help organise product suggestions, but it does not add prescription dispensing. A hotel option covers specified stay and room work, not every property-management or accounting function.

Why use it?

  • A consistent sale record: products, quantities, payment method, customer details when supplied, and receipt are captured in a controlled flow.
  • Stock visibility: supported product sales, receiving, adjustments, and returns can be reflected in stock records.
  • Workflows that fit different trading patterns: a walk-in checkout is different from an unpaid food order, an open table bill, or a room stay. Stockroom gives these work types distinct screens.
  • Business-specific language: owners can select templates and customise many labels and catalogue choices to fit their goods and staff vocabulary.
  • Accountability: roles and permissions can separate owner configuration from cashier work; reports and histories help review recorded activity.
  • A path from simple to more organised: begin with one workspace and add others as the business is ready.

What it is not

Stockroom is not automatically a full accounting package, prescription system, property-management suite, online ticket marketplace, production scheduler, or universal hardware driver. Choose the app for supported daily workflows, configure it honestly, and pilot it with real devices and staff before relying on it.

Chapter 2: Business type, workspace, and catalogue: three separate choices

These three terms answer different questions:

ChoiceQuestion answeredExample
Business typeWhat kind of business is this, and what defaults or starter names may help?Bakery and confectionery
WorkspaceWhat kind of operational screen should the staff use?Order counter for counter service; Product sales for stocked retail
CatalogueWhat actual items, services, ingredients, units, categories, prices, and stock does this business use?Agege bread, 1 loaf, price ₦X, opening stock Y

Choosing “Hotel” does not enable hotel work. Choosing “Bakery” does not configure recipes. Choosing “Wholesale” does not automatically create case conversions or bulk rates. Owners separately choose workspaces in Business settings > Available workspaces, then configure the real catalogue and settings.

First-run starter selection

During new-business setup, choose the business type and selling workspace separately. Search the suggested names, tick only relevant entries, review the selection, and choose Save and confirm workspace. You can go back to selection, skip starters, or add/edit later. The saved names are drafts: stock products begin at zero stock/cost/price and no barcode, service descriptions at zero price, and menu items at zero price and unavailable. They are not ready to sell until real business data is entered.

Editable wording and workspace-specific lists

In Business settings, workspace catalogue controls let an owner choose the workspace whose category and unit suggestions are being edited. Those category/unit lists are stored separately for stock-selling workspaces. Other (please specify) lets the owner enter a missing category or unit. Saved new choices can appear in the same workspace’s dropdown later. Product form custom fields are a separate configuration from business type and workspace selection.

Chapter 4: Plan the business before opening the app

Write down the following before setup:

  1. Trading identity: public business name, owner email, country, currency, address, branches, and contact details.
  2. Business type: the closest useful suggestion template. If none is exact, use General purpose or a related type and customise the catalogue.
  3. Workflows: list each way money or stock moves. A product checkout, service payment, table tab, and room stay are distinct examples.
  4. Catalogue source: product/service/menu names, categories, units, SKU/barcode if used, cost, selling price, opening stock, reorder/expiry details where applicable.
  5. Payments: cash, bank transfer, POS provider(s), any allowed customer extras/credit and the receipt wording your business needs.
  6. People: owner, managers/admins, cashiers, cooks/servers, receptionists, and what each role must be allowed to do.
  7. Devices: phone/tablet/computer, scanner, receipt printer, POS terminal, customer display, and internet availability for first connection and synchronization.
  8. Training transaction: a clear practice sale, its receipt, stock effect, and the permitted correction/return path.

For classroom work, use fictional details and a training business. A saved transaction is a real record, even when used in a lesson.

Chapter 5: Register, sign in, and complete first-time setup

New business

  1. Open the Stockroom welcome/sign-in journey and choose the registration request for a new business. Enter business details and the owner email; retrieve the registration key sent to that email and note its expiry.
  2. Choose New business with a key. Enter the issued key and matching owner email, then create the owner password and complete registration while connected.
  3. At workspace setup, choose Business type using its searchable picker. Type a word such as “bakery” and select the matching result.
  4. Choose Selling workspace from its dropdown. This is a distinct choice: Product sales, Oil sales, Payments & receipts, Order counter, or Tables & tabs.
  5. Choose the currency from the currency dropdown. The initial list includes the current currency and common options; use the one in which the business records prices.
  6. If adding a POS payment method, tick Add a POS payment method and enter the bank/provider name in the revealed input. Leave it unticked if not needed; owner settings can be revisited later.
  7. Search starter names, select suitable entries using their checkboxes, and review the category/unit shown with each name. Choose Continue to review; use Back to selection to change the list.
  8. Read the confirmation note. Choose Save and confirm workspace. Then edit every starter to enter accurate business data before selling.

Existing business on another device

Choose Existing business, enter the existing owner email/password, give the device a recognisable label such as “Front counter tablet”, and choose Connect this device. Do not register a second business to add a device. A new device needs connectivity to download the business data.

Passwords and access

Keep the owner password private. Create staff accounts after the owner configuration is saved, then grant each employee only the workspaces and actions required for their job.

Chapter 6: Navigate the app and understand roles

The navigation is grouped around the work:

  • Daily work: Overview, enabled selling workspaces, and Cash register.
  • Records and management: inventory/catalogue, sales history, customers, reports, staff, settings, and other tools shown for that business and role.
  • Business settings: focused configuration tasks. Use the searchable task selector to jump to a task such as Business identity, Payment methods, Branches, Available workspaces, Workspace catalogues, Product form, Receipts, or Devices.
  • Help: contextual guidance beside features and the searchable, downloadable in-app guide.

Before a sale, check the selected branch in the top branch selector. On a small screen, open the navigation menu (menu icon) to reveal the same destinations. In a searchable picker, type part of the label, choose the result, and confirm the selected value is displayed. A task picker navigates; a separate Save button commits changes.

Owners manage the business and subscriptions. Admins can configure what their permissions allow. Cashiers work in permitted selling screens. Some settings and actions may be hidden if the account does not have the relevant role or workspace.

Chapter 7: Set the business identity, branches, and payment methods

  1. Open Business settings and use the task selector to choose Business or Business identity.
  2. Enter the business/app name and choose the currency. Review the displayed currency before entering catalogue prices.
  3. Under payment methods, select the banks/POS providers staff will use. If your provider is absent, use the add-provider control, type its name, and save. Staff choose from the owner-enabled options at payment time. Provider order affects the default selection.
  4. Upload the logo if wanted. Review the app’s derived theme and use the appearance toggle for light/dark display if needed.
  5. In Shop branches, enter the branch name and optional address, then add/save. For an existing branch use Edit details or Staff access as needed. Use the top branch selector to switch the active branch before trading.
  6. Save business settings. Use Sync now when the configuration should be shared with connected devices.
  7. Read the expandable help before turning on customer credit or retained-extra-payment rules. These options change payment expectations and should be explained to staff.

Payment method picker at checkout

Cashiers select a method from the configured choices, such as Cash, POS Terminal, or Bank Transfer. Manual POS records retain provider/reference and evidence controls where shown. A connected terminal has its own send/verification controls. A payment-method selection is not a subscription payment: the former is what your customer pays your business; the latter pays for Stockroom access.

Chapter 8: Choose and save workspaces

  1. Open Business settings and select Available workspaces from the task search/picker.
  2. Tick each required option: Product sales, Oil sales, Payments & receipts, Order counter, Tables & tabs, Hotel operations, and/or Nightlife operations.
  3. Read the helper text for the selected controls. Hotel and Nightlife are separate switches under one Hospitality entitlement and open the Hospitality destination. Tables & tabs remains a different workflow for table bills.
  4. Choose Save workspaces. If subscription enforcement reports that access must be added, follow the displayed plan/checkout prompts, return to Available workspaces after payment, and save the selection. Payment alone does not enable the pending workspace.
  5. Confirm the enabled workspace cards appear on Overview and that a staff test account has the required permission.

Payments & receipts is the free workspace. The app’s displayed plan and access status are the source for current prices, trials, and entitlement. Changing the business type is a separate action and does not silently enable or disable workspaces. Existing records remain when a workspace is turned off.

Chapter 9: Configure sales, receipts, tax, and customer payment rules

Open Business settings, then use the focused task selector to choose Sales, Receipts, or the relevant task. Labels can differ with owner customisation.

Sales and pricing defaults

Review the sale/tax controls and set only rules the business understands. If tax calculation is enabled, enter its label and rate and choose whether displayed prices include tax or tax is added. Set product-specific rates when needed. Confirm price, discount, extra charges, and payment totals using an example before live trading.

Receipt identity

In Receipts, fill the business-facing receipt name and details offered by the screen. Configure the footer or receipt note if present. Save, then print/share a test receipt and check the business identity, date, items/descriptions, quantities, amounts, payment method, and total.

Customer extras, credit, and delivery

Use the explicit payment-rule settings and read their help before enabling a retained extra payment or credit. For takeaway delivery, configure the delivery option and fixed charge in Business settings if the business uses it. Test the customer-facing amount and receipt. Do not enable a rule merely because a screen offers it.

Chapter 10: Add and organise products, ingredients, services, and menus

Product entry and catalogue

Open product management (its name may be Inventory, Menu and stock, or an owner-defined label). Choose Add product or the equivalent. Complete the visible fields for name, category, unit, barcode/SKU, cost, selling price, opening quantity and any product-specific fields. Required fields are marked by the form. Use the category/unit dropdowns for saved choices; choose Other (please specify) and type a new value when the right one is missing. Review the workspace label so that you are editing the intended workspace’s list.

Use a real barcode from the package if applicable. An automatically generated SKU is an internal identifier, not a manufacturer barcode. Enter actual opening stock and cost; do not mistake starter placeholders for inventory. Save and inspect the product row/details before receiving stock or selling.

Variants and extras

Where a product needs sizes/colours or optional add-ons, open product variants/options and select the product, group/variant label, option names, and any additional prices. Save and check that the selling screen presents the correct options. Business type alone does not create sizes, colours, extras, or serial-number handling.

Purchasing and receiving

Record purchases through the purchasing/receiving workflow. Search for the item or supplier as the screen allows, enter the quantities/costs and any batch/expiry details used by the business, review the receive action, and save. Use stock count/adjustment for a verified count difference; use wastage/expiry controls for the relevant loss record. Check the resulting stock ledger/history.

Menu and recipes

Order counter and Tables & tabs have separately configured menus/catalogues. Open the matching menu task, add/edit a menu item, set a real price, and mark it available only when it can be offered. Link stock items and recipe quantities where the screen offers them. Save, then open the actual selling workspace and verify the item appears. If using a production batch, check the ingredient/material deductions and actual yield; the feature is single-output batch tracking, not a production scheduler.

Custom business: direct selection or adaptation

If your exact business is absent, choose General purpose for mixed goods, Services and non-stock items for service descriptions, or the closest operational type. For example, a phone repair shop can use Product sales for accessories and spare parts and Payments & receipts > Jobs & invoices for repair work. The label does not create a device repair database or warranty history.

Chapter 12: Product sales: retail, wholesale, supermarket, pharmacy, and general goods

  1. Select the correct branch and open Product sales.
  2. Find the item by scrolling/searching or scan its barcode. Confirm the exact product name and price before adding.
  3. Tap/click the product to add it to the basket. Adjust quantity in the basket; verify the unit and available stock.
  4. Use the optional customer/discount/note section only when applicable. Search/select an existing customer or enter the requested new customer details. Review discount and notes.
  5. Choose Take payment. Check the amount, select payment method, and complete any provider/reference/receipt evidence controls shown for that method.
  6. Choose Complete sale. Wait for the saved confirmation/receipt, then print/share if offered.
  7. Confirm the receipt in Sales history and the stock change in inventory for a stock-controlled item.

If the wrong item or amount appears, stop before completing payment and correct the basket. For a saved sale, use the authorised return/refund/correction workflow; do not simply change the current product price expecting an old receipt to change.

Weighted goods

Supported weighing uses keyboard-output scale readings or a specified weight-label format. Products must use kg stock and price per kg. Configure the device and product mapping, then test stable readings, tare, label checksum, quantity precision, stock shortage, receipt, and return with the actual hardware. Stockroom does not control tare/stability and does not support every scale serial protocol.

Chapter 13: Oil sales: measured quantity, containers, and customer rates

Enable Oil sales separately from the oil business type. Add oil products with the real base unit, container/handling supplies where sold, and correct stock. In the Oil sales workspace, search or scan the oil product, enter measured quantity or choose a configured container, and verify the displayed quantity and rate label. Customer-specific or bulk threshold pricing may apply; compare the rate and total shown before taking payment. Finish payment and save the receipt. Review the stock ledger afterward.

Configure and test rate thresholds, customer precedence, manual overrides, decimal precision, refunds, and container charges using non-live practice records before launch. This workflow handles sales quantities and configured prices; it does not connect to a pump or meter.

Chapter 14: Payments & receipts: services, church collections, jobs, and invoices

Record a manual payment

Open Payments & receipts and choose the receipt/payment entry. Add an item description, quantity, and unit price. Use Add another item for each separate line. Optionally open Customer and receipt details to enter customer name, phone, or transaction type. Choose the payment method, review total and receipt details, then save. Print or share the saved receipt and use payment history to find it later.

This workflow records described value and does not deduct stock. If stock was sold, use a stock-selling workspace.

Jobs & invoices

Use the Jobs & invoices task for estimates, work progress, invoices, deposits, and balance payments. Search/select the customer where appropriate, enter job description and agreed amounts, save the estimate/job, and update its status as work progresses. Record each payment against the correct job and inspect the balance before issuing a final receipt/statement. Record materials and costs when the tool offers them; issuing a receipt by itself does not automatically consume materials.

Church collections

Open Church collections, configure the funds and collection categories actually used, and select/create donor records according to the screen. Record the received contribution, date, fund, amount, and payment detail; save and confirm its receipt/statement. Use the relevant recorded refund or correction path for mistakes. The tool covers funds, donors, pledges and received contributions; it is not a full general ledger or fund-spending system.

Chapter 15: Order counter: fast food, bakery, catering, and food batches

New order

  1. Open Order counter and select menu items; search if the menu is long.
  2. Set quantity and choose any configured variant or extras. Add each item to the order and verify prices and notes.
  3. Open optional customer/order details to enter the customer/account and preparation note.
  4. Choose Send and take payment to save and proceed to payment, or Send for preparation when payment will occur later.
  5. Choose a configured payment method, confirm the amount, and complete payment when due. The saved order can be followed in Orders and Preparation.

Preparation, collection, and corrections

Use Preparation to review order progress; use Orders for payment and handover. Update statuses as staff prepare and serve the order. When the payment form is open, order entry is hidden to reduce accidental changes; use Close payment to return to the queue. Use Order actions for permitted corrections, cancellation, and refunds. Existing reasons, roles, and consumed-stock safeguards apply.

Optional food batch

Configure ingredients and a recipe, then use the supported single-output batch tool to record the actual quantities consumed and actual output yield/cost. Review stock before and after. It does not schedule production or calculate multiple co-products automatically.

Chapter 16: Tables & tabs: restaurants, bars, and nightlife bills

Configure this workspace’s menu and table list separately. Open Tables & tabs, choose a table or open a named tab, and add menu items/rounds to its bill. Verify the item lines and total; use bill actions to move or merge where authorised. To settle, choose Take payment. Whole-bill payment is the starting choice; use seat, item, or shared-payment choices if needed. Select a payment method and save. Print the receipt.

Choose Close bill and free table only after payment and after all items are served or cancelled. The button’s eligibility follows those conditions. History contains prior bills; use the authorised refund or bill correction flow if necessary. During payment, new-order entry is hidden; close payment to return to the table workflow.

This is appropriate for repeated rounds and open customer tabs. Hotel/nightlife event reservations may link to these tabs when both workflows are enabled, but event admission and table settlement remain distinct records.

Chapter 17: Hospitality operations: hotel and nightlife

In Available workspaces, turn on Hotel operations, Nightlife operations, or both. They open Hospitality operations and share one entitlement. Overview and the destination reflect which option(s) are enabled.

Hotel operations

Configure rooms and their identifiers, then check the availability calendar for the intended dates. Create the guest/stay record with the requested dates and details, review room availability, and save the booking/status. Update the stay as the guest checks in/out. Assign housekeeping staff or schedule where the workflow offers it; record readiness. Add supported folio/extra charges or stocked product charges to the appropriate stay/invoice, then review and settle the invoice. Use customer booking/QR check-in where configured.

The implementation includes room register, availability, stay status, housekeeping readiness/assignment/schedule, invoices and folio charges. It is not a full property-management system: do not assume rate/channel management, housekeeping task history/workload boards, or accounting integrations.

Nightlife operations

Create an event with its date, capacity and event information. Add or search guests on the guest list, create admission invoices/payment records, assign a host/door lead where offered, and use QR check-in at entry. Configure customer event booking links where used. If tables/tabs are also enabled, configure tables/menu/bills there and connect event reservations/tabs to the event. Admission is separate from food/drink table billing.

The app supports event/guest/admission/check-in workflows. It is not an integrated ticket marketplace or membership/loyalty system. Confirm online collection and customer notifications are connected before promoting a booking link.

Chapter 18: Devices, staff, branches, and day-to-day controls

Device setup

Open Business settings > Devices or the Devices task. Configure only hardware actually in use: barcode scanner, POS terminal/provider, receipt printer, customer display, camera scanning, or weighed-goods method. Follow the on-screen connection/pairing instructions for that device and complete a small test. Device setup does not automatically add a POS provider to the permitted payment list; the owner configures providers separately under Business.

Staff and access

Create a staff account in Staff & access using the available name/login/role inputs. Open Access permissions, select the staff member, tick the required workspace/actions, and save with the owner password where requested. Have the staff member sign in and verify that they see the correct screens. New staff may need permissions before they can operate. Synchronize so other devices receive access changes.

Cash register

Open Cash register at the start of the trading period. Enter opening cash, save, record each cash movement with its description/category/amount, and at close enter counted cash. Review the expected-versus-counted difference and save the close. Cash register entries support cash accountability; they do not replace bank reconciliation or full accounting.

Sync and backups

Use Sync now to upload pending work and download updates. A refresh that only downloads should not be mistaken for a full sync. Review Sync status/issues and resolve conflicts against the affected record. On Windows, Business backup/restore offers encrypted backups and optional scheduled backups; restoring requires file password, owner password, and the explicit RESTORE confirmation. Treat backups as a recovery copy and keep credentials safe.

Chapter 19: Trading day: opening, selling, closing, and correcting records

Opening checklist

  • Confirm the branch selector, staff login, and allowed workspace.
  • Confirm the device’s scanner/printer/payment configuration and connectivity state.
  • For cash trading, open the cash register and record opening float.
  • Review menu availability or product stock for items that will be offered.
  • Confirm that the receipt displays the correct business identity and currency.

Close and review

  • Finish or hand over open orders, table bills, stays, and admissions according to their workflow.
  • Count and close the cash register; explain differences using the app’s records and approved business procedure.
  • Review sales history, payment history, stock movement, and any relevant report.
  • Synchronize connected devices and check unresolved sync issues.

Correcting errors

Before saving, correct a wrong item, quantity, customer, or payment method in the draft. After a saved transaction, use the appropriate authorised void, return, refund, cancellation, or adjustment flow. Preserve the original record and reason. A refund should follow the transaction’s saved amounts; changing current prices is not a correction to historical sales.

12. Devices and Product sales > Weighed goods

  • Owners/admins open Business settings > Devices > Weighed goods hardware. Enable keyboard-output readings or weight labels on this checkout device. Products must use kg stock and a price per kg.

  • Take payment through normal checkout. Receipt quantity and stock deduction use the measured kg. Multiple confirmed parcels accumulate; refunds use the saved receipt quantities and prices.
  • Test a real scale with reference weights, tare, stability, disconnects, barcode errors, a receipt and a return. This implementation supports keyboard output and the specified label format; serial protocols and live polling need model-specific adapters.

Scope note: No scale model has been physically verified here. Device settings are local. The app does not control tare or stability. A price-encoded label cannot be treated as a weight label; configure the exact supported layout.

  • Open Product sales. Scan a product barcode or tap a product tile to add it to the basket.

Scope note: Use Back to products to change the unpaid basket. Hold sale saves a basket for later; Resume sale restores it. Clear basket clears an unpaid draft. A connected POS request must be resolved before altering its locked basket.

19. Product and oil sales: retail, bulk and customer prices

  • Use Product sales for ordinary stocked goods, or the wholesale/retail oil setup for measured oils. Add each product in a consistent base unit with its retail selling price and purchase cost.
  • Open Purchasing and receiving > Pack / receiving conversions, add a named pack and its base-unit factor, enable its checkout offering and enter its selling price. Selling packs work in Product sales and containers work in Oil sales. Loose-unit tiers do not require packs.
  • Owners/admins open Inventory > Product price tiers (Oil price tiers for oil businesses), choose a product, and add bulk minimum quantities and prices per base stock unit. The highest qualifying threshold applies to the combined quantity of that product in Product sales or Oil sales; configured selling packs and oil containers contribute their base-unit quantity.
  • Add customer rates by selecting existing customer accounts. Select that customer in the checkout basket to apply its rate. Customer rate takes priority over bulk; otherwise retail/product or retail/container prices apply. Explicit manual prices and custom extra lines retain their separate prices.
  • Review rate labels, quantities and total before taking payment. Quantity or customer changes can change the applicable price. Saved receipts retain the selected price and label; refunds use those saved amounts.
  • Example: retail 10 per litre, bulk 8 from 20 litres and 7 from 100 litres, customer rate 6. One 25-litre can costs 200 under bulk pricing; one can plus 75 loose litres costs 700, or 600 with that customer selected. Stock consumption is 100 litres.
  • Save tiers with the existing sync workflow and update participating apps. Refresh before shared checkout to load changed rates. A stale settings save requires review; drafts preserve command IDs for retrying interrupted saves. Old app versions do not calculate these rates.

Scope note: Rates are per base stock unit, not per container. Bulk rates apply to the entire qualifying quantity, not only units above the threshold. Different products do not combine. The same saved product rules apply in Product sales and Oil sales. Service descriptions and prepared-food menus retain their own prices; these rules do not reprice those workflows.

  • Mark arrived, then Open reservation bill from 30 minutes before arrival until departure. Mark seated links that bill. Mark visit completed does not settle or close it; use the normal bill payment and closing controls.
  • Cancel reservation or Mark no-show requires confirmation and a reason. Owners/admins can Archive finished reservations without deleting history, or Export reservation history as CSV summaries. Reservations do not collect deposits or record sales.

Scope note: A printed unpaid bill is not a payment receipt. Synchronize devices before sharing table occupancy; use one till to manage each open bill. Shared reservations require updated apps and a sync backend supporting restaurant-reservations-v1. Synchronize before confirming a table; review offline calendar conflicts in Sync issues.

27. Tables & tabs > Take payment and close a bill

  • Open the bill and select Take payment. New-order entry is hidden while payment is open.
  • Use the default whole remaining bill, or choose the offered seat, item/quantity or equal-share selection.
  • Choose cash, POS, transfer or mixed payment. Enter tender, provider/reference and payment parts as required.
  • Save bill payment. Print the saved receipt. Remaining unpaid quantities stay on the bill for later settlement.
  • Once the bill is fully paid and every order is served or cancelled, select Close bill and free table.

Scope note: If the bill changed during payment entry, review it and use Refresh payment entry. Retrying the same saved payment must not create another charge. Close payment entry returns to adding orders.

28. Tables & tabs > Bill actions, History and refunds

  • Expand Bill actions on an open bill to move it to another free table or merge an eligible unpaid bill into another.
  • Choose the destination and confirm. Verify table occupancy and the combined bill afterwards.
  • Open History to find Restaurant payment receipts and Closed bills.
  • Reprint the payment receipt or closed bill. For an authorised refund, choose returned quantities and the actual refund method/reference.
  • Review the remaining bill/return history after saving.

Scope note: Move/merge operations retain original orders and prices. Merge is restricted to compatible unpaid bills on the original till/currency. Refunds do not automatically restock prepared items.

29. Hospitality operations: hotels and nightlife

  • In Business settings > Available workspaces, turn on Hotel operations, Nightlife operations, or both. They are separate options that use one Hospitality subscription entitlement and one Hospitality operations destination. Overview labels the destination for the option(s) enabled.
  • For a hotel, add rooms with nightly rates and keep room status current. Book stays by room and dates; overlapping bookings are rejected and the monthly availability calendar shows room stays.
  • Assign a stay manager and housekeeping staff from existing staff who have Hotel operations access in this branch. Set room readiness and a cleaning time. A room must be ready before check-in; checking out marks it dirty.
  • Create the stay invoice from the booking and use Payments & receipts > Jobs & invoices to record deposits, balances and receipts. During an active stay, add priced folio charges or charge stocked products. Stock is deducted when the stocked-product invoice is created. Check-out is blocked while stay/folio invoices have a balance due.
  • For nightlife, create an event with its time, optional capacity and admission price. Assign an event host and door lead from staff who have Nightlife operations access. Add guests; paid entries need an invoice paid in full before check-in. Free entries can be checked in without an invoice.
  • Customer-created hotel and event bookings show a QR pass in the customer portal. Use Scan guest QR or Scan admission QR at the venue; staff-entered bookings can be checked in with the staff action. Passes are single-use. Customer booking requires an internet connection.
  • If Tables & tabs is enabled, event table reservations and venue tabs link to the nightlife event. Configure tables, menus and bills in Tables & tabs; that workspace continues to handle food, drinks and settlement.
  • Guests can make bookings through the customer portal as a guest or sign in to link them to a customer account. The portal shows booking and invoice balances but does not collect the hotel/admission invoice payment; staff record payment in Jobs & invoices. A linked customer wallet can be used by staff when enabled by payment policy.

Scope note: Hospitality reuses existing staff accounts, branch permissions, Jobs & invoices, stock, customer accounts and Tables & tabs. It is not a full hotel PMS or ticketing/accounting system. There is no separate hospitality staff directory, housekeeping task history/workload board, direct online invoice payment, or ticket marketplace.

30. Cash register

  • Open Cash register at the start of the shift. Enter Starting cash and Open register.
  • Record only separate cash-in/cash-out movements, entering amount and reason.
  • Cash sales, cash refunds and supported supplier cash transactions recorded during the session are counted automatically.
  • At shift end, count physical cash, enter Cash counted at closing and explain a difference when required.
  • Select Close register and review expected cash, counted cash and the difference.
  • If an entry is interrupted, retry with its original values. After reloading, use Refresh and recover register entries to check whether it was saved.
  • For staff handover, close and count the outgoing shift on its original till before the next staff member opens a shift. Owners/admins can use Review staff shift on this till to close an outgoing staff shift.

Scope note: Do not record a cash sale again as a manual cash movement. The cash register is daily work, not configuration.

31. Sales history: receipts, voids and customer history

  • Open Sales history and use its section links to find Receipt history or Voided items.
  • Choose the saved receipt and reprint it. Use digital receipt controls where offered.
  • Use Return items from a completed receipt for authorised returns: choose quantities, stock restoration only for resellable goods, method and reason.
  • Review customer history and balances for the selected customer.
  • Use Payment evidence and Match payments to review provider/reference records or reconcile an imported provider report.

Scope note: A payment reference, OCR suggestion or imported report needs review; it is not a new payment. Preserve original receipts and use the return process instead of rewriting history.

32. Stock count and Stock movements

  • Open Stock count, start/select a count and enter the quantities physically found.
  • Review the difference from recorded stock. Submit/review approval using the available authorised controls.
  • Open Stock movements to trace recorded stock changes and reasons.
  • For an incorrect quantity, use the appropriate stock adjustment, count, receipt or transfer action rather than altering a historical sale.

Scope note: Permissions determine who may approve corrections. Approved losses affect stock/cost reporting; do not duplicate them as unrelated expenses.

33. Customer display

  • On a supported desktop, open Customer display or its device setup.
  • For a second monitor, extend the desktop and use Open customer display on second monitor.
  • For a browser customer device, connect it to the same private shop Wi-Fi and create a customer share link.
  • Open the link on the intended customer device while the pairing link is valid.
  • Add products in Product sales and confirm the live basket, total and completed-sale confirmation on the customer screen.

Scope note: The display is read-only. Current native/mobile-browser builds do not offer the same desktop display integration. Pairing/session expiry instructions are shown in the app.

34. Customer accounts

  • Open Customer accounts and select or add the customer with their name and contact details.
  • Review their balance and recorded account activity.
  • Use the available account controls to record funds or repayments with the correct amount and reference.
  • When wallet payments are enabled, choose the customer wallet during checkout and review the available balance.
  • Only the owner may approve a purchase that creates debt when credit is enabled.
  • Open Birthday reminder on a customer account, enter MM-DD and enable the reminder. Save locally, then sync to receive cloud notifications. Staff with Customer accounts access receive a reminder once per customer/date in the business timezone; greetings are sent manually.

Scope note: Customer balances are separate from owner referral rewards. Account entries record business transactions; they are not a bank transfer initiated by the app.

35. Overview and Reports

  • On Overview, expand Business performance for synced sales and stock metrics without adding cards to the daily work view.
  • Open Reports to review sales, stock valuation, estimated profit and low-stock items.
  • Record actual running expenses with category, description, amount and date.
  • Use the offered report export/print action for the current data.
  • Review missing costs before relying on the profit estimate.

Scope note: Reports use saved records. Prepared items without recipes have untracked ingredient costs. The performance view is platform-dependent; do not enter purchased stock again as a running expense. Reports use the saved business reporting timezone shown on the report; date-only expenses keep their entered business date. Synchronize settings and records before comparing devices. If Profit estimate has incomplete costs appears, review the named prepared items, saved recipes and purchase costs before trusting profit. Delivery charges are excluded from recipe warnings. Enter running expenses separately.

36. Staff & access and Staff activity

  • Use the Staff & access task selector to move between Team members, Access permissions, Add staff, Sign-in links, and Reset staff password when available. These are separate tasks; Team members and Access permissions are not duplicate views.
  • As owner, open Access permissions, select a staff member and tick the workspaces and operations they need. Save using your owner password. New staff cannot operate until access is granted. Existing access remains until you save a selection; offline devices receive changes on their next sync.
  • Open Add staff, enter the requested details and temporary password, then confirm the username and owner credentials when prompted. Give the staff member their username and temporary password securely.
  • Use Reset staff password for recovery. To revoke access, use Remove staff beside an admin or cashier in Team members, read the warning, and enter your owner password. Cancel makes no change; the red Confirm staff removal button submits the request. Synchronize other devices afterward.
  • Open Staff activity to select branch/person/date range and review recorded sales, expenses and voids.

Scope note: Staff creation/recovery requires the appropriate online owner verification. Cashiers use their staff username; the owner uses the registered email. Only owners can remove staff. Removal preserves sales and activity history, blocks cloud access immediately, and reaches offline devices when they reconnect and sync. Removed usernames remain reserved for historical identity. Admin access does not automatically grant every owner setting.

37. Sync now and Sync issues

  • Connect to the internet and check the displayed sync readiness for this device.
  • Press Sync now to share queued changes and receive accepted updates.
  • If changes need review, open Sync issues, select the affected record and inspect both saved versions. Check current records and use Stock count, Sales history or the relevant workspace to correct the discrepancy.
  • Use the available authorised resolution/retry control, then synchronize again.
  • Check the relevant workspace/history after synchronization before continuing shared-device work.

Scope note: Local saving is separate from synchronization. A disabled sync control or incompatible-server message cannot be solved by repeatedly creating transactions. Do not switch stock allocations while tills remain offline.

38. Close your account, exports and referrals

  • Open Subscription for owner referral links, rewards and payout controls where available. Customer accounts are a different balance system.
  • Before leaving, use the offered export controls and check that you can open the downloaded files.
  • Only owners can open Close business account. Choose Delete business account to read the warning screen. Choose Cancel to leave without submitting, or type DELETE and use the red Confirm account deletion button when ready. Staff cannot close their accounts.
  • To stop future subscription renewal, use Manage or cancel subscription on the Subscription screen or the app-store subscription settings for an Android purchase. Closing a business account and cancelling renewal are separate actions.

Scope note: Closure deactivates cloud access immediately and schedules deletion after the displayed waiting period (14 days by default). Cancel before the deadline to restore access. Export necessary records and manage recurring subscriptions separately. Offline device copies are not remotely erased.

39. End-of-day checklist and when help is needed

  • Finish or identify outstanding payments. Review open orders and open table bills. Check Jobs & invoices for outstanding service balances.
  • Print/reprint any required receipts. Confirm returns and separate cash movements are recorded once.
  • Count and close the cash register. Review differences and the day's sales.
  • Synchronize when connected and review unresolved sync issues. Log out when handing the device to another user.
  • Contact the app provider for unavailable release downloads, failed registration delivery, unavailable provider integration or persistent service problems. Contact the device supplier for printer-driver or hardware issues.
  • For the first supervised pilot, use a separate practice business first. Record opening stock, purchase costs, recipes and an opening cash float. Agree who will check the cash, bank evidence and physical stock.
  • Run cash, bank transfer, split payment, a correction and a refund. For food businesses, submit a QR pickup and a delivery order, verify the bank details and fee, and check kitchen/bar tickets from a second device.
  • Disconnect the internet, record an ordinary local sale, reconnect and synchronize. Confirm one receipt, one stock deduction and no duplicate ticket. QR submissions and shared printing require connectivity.
  • Close and count the register. Compare cash expected, counted cash, transfers, refunds, stock usage and estimated profit with an independent paper tally. Investigate every unexplained difference.
  • Download an encrypted backup and check the scheduled backup folder. In the practice business, test restore and an enrolled replacement till recovery. Do not replace live records for a rehearsal. Begin the real-business pilot only after these checks pass, with someone available to help staff.

Scope note: Ordinary sales, menus, tables, receipt settings and renewals have in-app controls. Hosting, release publishing, mail delivery and payment-provider provisioning remain service-operator tasks, not instructions for a shop owner to edit code.

40. Support, reconciliation and recovery updates

  • Find support email, information email and WhatsApp links in How to use the app. Report the device and error; keep passwords and payment-card details private.
  • In Sync issues, inspect both versions and verify current records after synchronization. Correct discrepancies through normal stock/payment/configuration tools; select an outcome, provide an explanation and confirm the check before Record review outcome. Local outcome history does not rewrite business records or synchronize to other devices.
  • In sales reconciliation, select External POS or Bank transfer, map statement columns, select currency and covered UTC sale dates. Split portions are compared individually. Export the comparison and investigate absent, duplicate, unsuccessful or mismatched payments. Synchronize and load relevant receipts first.
  • New Windows backups support compatible additive database updates. Older archives without schema metadata can be upgraded automatically for recognised field additions when their original structure can be verified.

Scope note: Statement matches are not live bank verification. Compare gross payments; fees and refunds are not netted. Contact support before attempting an unsupported backup migration.

41. Copy takeaway and restaurant menus

  • Owners/admins open the destination menu in Business settings. Enable both workspaces and synchronize before loading the source.
  • Open Copy menu items from restaurant/takeaway, preview the source and select items. Review prices, ingredient quantities and extras.
  • Choose Add selected items to menu draft, review stock links, availability and stations, then Save menu. Cancel preview changes no menu; Discard menu changes removes unsaved additions.

Scope note: Existing items stay unchanged. Same-name and previously copied source items are blocked. Copies have fresh identities and independent future edits; submitted orders retain their snapshots.

42. Online retail ordering and delivery

  • As owner, open Business settings > Business. Enable online retail ordering and choose the products to publish. Save business settings and synchronize. Only selected products with positive selling prices are published; costs and stock counts stay private.
  • Share the retail customer link under Business sign-in links, or its QR code. Mixed food/retail businesses use the retail link for stock products and the existing food link for menus.
  • Configure published bank details and optional fixed delivery charge or delivery-area prices. Customers can order as guests, request delivery and submit transfer references without Paystack verification. A transfer reference is a claim; staff must verify receipt of money.
  • Open Online retail orders on the fulfilment branch (currently Main branch). Sign in as staff granted Product sales access, or Oil sales for an oil order. Accept the order while connected and sync it to this till.
  • Use the Picking queue to start picking and mark ready. Take payment on the accepted till. For delivery, assign an agent, mark ready, Dispatch delivery, then Complete delivery on arrival. Pickup orders use Hand over. Payment uses existing cash, transfer, terminal, split and owner-enabled wallet controls; stock is deducted once when payment is recorded.
  • After acceptance and sync, picking and payment save locally through an outage. Sync when connected to update customer tracking. Online requests do not reserve stock; check actual availability before confirming payment.

Scope note: Retail online prices are the published product retail prices in base units. Walk-in pack, bulk and customer pricing remain in the selling workspaces. Online ordering and remote notifications need an internet connection; ordinary checkout remains offline first.

43. Contact support through Gmail

  • Open How to use the app > Contact Stockroom support > Send a support request. Enter your name, contact email, subject and what happened.
  • Drafts are saved on this device while offline. Press Send support request when connected. Keep the displayed reference.
  • If sending is interrupted, use Retry saved request. It retains the same reference. Support replies to your contact email; email and WhatsApp links remain available.

Scope note: The form requires an enrolled device and internet to send. It does not read your Gmail inbox. Do not include passwords or payment-card details.
